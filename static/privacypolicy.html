Self Improvement Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Local data

Self Improvement has no account system, application backend, analytics, advertising, custom server, or AI service. Your profile, settings, tracker history, rewards, and manual nutrition entries are stored in the app on your device.

Android data

With your permission, the app reads step totals from Health Connect. It reads foreground app usage, foreground intervals, and screen-interactive times through Android Usage Access for screen-time and bedtime-adherence tracking. Access is read-only. The app does not write to Health Connect, inspect screen contents, or upload native tracker data.

Optional bedtime reminders are scheduled and displayed on your device. Nutrition is entered manually; the app does not request camera access or analyze photos.

App updates

The Android app checks the public Self Improvement release feed on GitHub for a newer signed version. This sends only a standard network request to GitHub; profile, tracker, nutrition, usage, and backup data are not included. An available update is downloaded only after you tap its action and confirm the native installation prompt.

Exports and Google Drive backups

You can explicitly export a JSON backup or restore a JSON backup through Android's document picker. You can also choose a Google Drive folder through Android's Storage Access Framework. If you choose one, the app can write one backup per day when it opens or resumes and keeps only its five newest timestamped backup files in that folder.

The installed Google Drive document provider handles transfer to your Google Drive account. Self Improvement does not receive Google credentials, access other Drive locations, or send backups through a custom server. Unrelated files in the selected folder are not rotated.

Your choices

You can revoke Health Connect, Usage Access, notification, app-installation, or selected-folder access in Android settings. Google Drive backup is optional. You can choose a different folder, export a backup manually, or keep all data only in the app.

Sharing and retention

Self Improvement does not sell or share personal, health, nutrition, or usage data. Local data remains until it is edited, deleted, replaced by a restore, or removed with the app's storage. User-selected Drive backup files remain subject to the backup retention described above and to your Google Drive settings.
