Self Improvement Companion Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Data the companion reads

With your permission, the Android companion reads step totals and sleep sessions, including sleep stages and source information, from Health Connect. It also reads daily foreground app usage from Android Usage Access. These permissions are read-only. The companion does not write to Health Connect.

How data is used

The companion sends the most recent seven local days to the Self Improvement server you select while pairing. The data is used only to populate your steps, sleep, and screen-time trackers. Package names are sent for screen-time entries; the companion does not request a list of all installed applications.

On-device security

Pairing credentials and synchronization status are encrypted with an Android Keystore-backed secure store. They are removed from this device when you disconnect the companion. Health and usage payloads are prepared in memory for upload and are not stored by the companion.

Sharing and retention

The companion does not sell health or usage data and does not send it to advertising or analytics services. Records uploaded to Self Improvement remain subject to the account and retention controls of that server.

Your choices

You can revoke Health Connect or Usage Access permissions in Android settings at any time. You can disconnect in the companion to erase its local connection. You can manage or delete uploaded records through your Self Improvement account.

Questions

Use the support contact provided by the Self Improvement server paired with this app.
