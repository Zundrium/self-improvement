Self Improvement Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026

Data the app handles

With your permission, the Android app reads step totals from Health Connect. It reads daily foreground app usage, detailed foreground intervals, and screen-interactive times from Android Usage Access for screen time and bedtime adherence. These permissions are read-only. The app does not write to Health Connect and does not read screen contents.

The app stores the tracker information you enter, including workouts, meals, meditation, breathing, happiness reasons, menstruation notes, profile settings, and account details. If you use nutrition photo analysis, the app uses the camera or a photo you select.

How data is used

The app sends native tracker data to your authenticated account at self.zund.cc. Package names are sent for screen-time and bedtime entries; the app does not request a list of all installed applications. Data is used to provide the tracker screens and persistent history you request.

Nutrition photos, estimates, and corrections are sent through OpenRouter to the configured AI model for meal analysis. Confirmed meal photos and nutrition results are stored in your account so they remain available in your food log.

On-device security

Your signed login session and synchronization status are encrypted with an Android Keystore-backed secure store. They are removed from this device when you sign out. Health and usage payloads are prepared in memory for upload and are not retained as local files.

Sharing and retention

Self Improvement does not sell personal, health, or usage data and does not send it to advertising or analytics services. Apart from nutrition analysis through OpenRouter, tracker data is not shared with third-party processing services. Uploaded records remain in your private account until they are changed or deleted through the app.

Your choices

You can revoke Health Connect, Usage Access, or notification permission in Android settings at any time. Bedtime reminders are optional and scheduled only on your device. You can sign out to erase the local session and manage your persistent records through the Self Improvement app.

Questions

Use the support contact provided for Self Improvement.
